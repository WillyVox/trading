```{=html}
<p>
```
A polished website, app-store listing or social-media following does not
prove that a crypto platform is legitimate. In 2026, Australians have a
useful check: AUSTRAC's public Virtual Asset Service Provider register.
Combine that with website, fee, withdrawal and scam checks before
sending money.
```{=html}
</p>
```
```{=html}
<h2>
```
Step 1: search the AUSTRAC VASP register
```{=html}
</h2>
```
```{=html}
<p>
```
AUSTRAC made its VASP register public on 30 June 2026. Search the
provider's legal or trading name and, where available, identifiers such
as its ABN or ACN. Make sure the result belongs to the business you
think you are dealing with.
```{=html}
</p>
```
```{=html}
<h2>
```
Step 2: understand what registration does --- and does not --- mean
```{=html}
</h2>
```
```{=html}
<p>
```
AUSTRAC registration is relevant to Australia's anti-money-laundering
framework. Do not turn that into a claim that every crypto asset or
service is "ASIC approved", guaranteed, safe or protected from loss.
Licensing requirements can depend on the specific financial product or
service.
```{=html}
</p>
```
```{=html}
<h2>
```
Step 3: verify the real website and app
```{=html}
</h2>
```
```{=html}
<p>
```
Scammers impersonate legitimate digital-asset platforms and create fake
websites and apps. Do not sign in through an unexpected message or
social-media link. Find the provider independently and check the domain
carefully.
```{=html}
</p>
```
```{=html}
<h2>
```
Step 4: read fees and withdrawal rules
```{=html}
</h2>
```
```{=html}
<p>
```
Find the provider's official fee information and learn how AUD and
crypto can be withdrawn before depositing. If a platform unexpectedly
demands an extra "unlock", "recovery" or similar payment before
releasing funds, stop and investigate.
```{=html}
</p>
```

```{=html}
<h2>
```
A 60-second check before sending money
```{=html}
</h2>
```
```{=html}
<p>
```
Ask: Am I on the real website? Can I find the provider in the relevant
Australian register? Do I understand fees and withdrawals? Did anyone
pressure me? Is anyone promising guaranteed returns? If an answer
worries you, stop and independently verify it.
