## How to make my website display at page 1 google search in Australia? My website name is trading

http://guide.com.au Analyse and explore comprehensively, give me feedback and concerns, then bullet points on how to achieve that ultimate goal?

## Answer

For TradingGuide.com.au, reaching page 1 in Australia is possible, but I would frame the goal differently:

Don't try to rank the homepage #1 for “trading.” Build TradingGuide into an Australian topical authority that owns hundreds of high-intent searches around brokers, trading platforms, fees, comparisons and investing education.

Google explicitly says there is no method that automatically guarantees a #1 ranking. For your site, the challenge is also harder because financial information falls into Google's YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) area, where Google says signals associated with strong experience, expertise, authoritativeness and especially trust receive greater weight.

Based on the TradingGuide product we've been developing—broker/exchange pages, comparisons, articles, affiliate links, market information and educational content—I would build the SEO strategy around that reality.

My assessment of TradingGuide.com.au

The domain itself is a good starting point.

TradingGuide immediately communicates the subject, while .com.au strongly aligns the brand with Australia. But the domain alone won't produce rankings. Google needs evidence that TradingGuide deserves to appear above established Australian financial publishers and comparison sites.

The biggest opportunity is actually not generic keywords such as:

trading
shares
crypto

Those searches are extremely broad.

Instead, your initial opportunity is much more specific commercial and informational searches:

5. Be extremely transparent about affiliate monetisation

Affiliate links themselves aren't the problem.

Trying to make an affiliate page look like independent analysis without adequate disclosure creates both trust and regulatory concerns.

For example:

How TradingGuide makes money
TradingGuide may receive compensation when users visit or sign up with certain providers through links on our website. This does not change the fees users pay or our published comparison methodology.

You should obtain Australian legal/compliance advice for the final wording and operating model.

ASIC's updated RG 234 applies to promoters and publishers advertising financial products and services and emphasizes avoiding false or misleading representations.

So SEO, affiliate monetisation and compliance should be designed together, not separately.

6. Build author authority

Anonymous AI-written finance articles are a poor long-term foundation for this particular site.

Each author should have a proper profile:

/about/authors/huy-vo

including relevant background, areas covered, articles written, research methodology and appropriate professional disclosures.

Don't manufacture qualifications or pretend someone has investment expertise they don't have.

For specialist financial subjects, you can also have appropriately qualified reviewers where necessary.

7. Don't execute the “1,000 AI pages” strategy

This is one of my largest concerns.

You previously explored scaling TradingGuide toward hundreds or thousands of SEO pages. That can work only when the pages provide substantive unique value.

Google specifically warns that using generative AI to create many pages without adding value can constitute scaled content abuse.

So don't generate:

Best broker Sydney
Best broker Melbourne
Best broker Brisbane
Best broker Perth
...

with essentially identical content.

And don't generate every permutation:

Create one authoritative canonical page per search intent.

10. Implement structured data

I'd implement at least:

Organization
Article
BreadcrumbList
Person/ProfilePage where appropriate

Don't add schema merely because a generator says you can. It needs to accurately represent visible content.

Google supports Article structured data and Organization markup can help Google understand and disambiguate the organisation and its logo.

Breadcrumb structured data can also help Google understand site hierarchy.

11. Performance matters

Target Google's "good" Core Web Vitals thresholds:

Metric Target
LCP ≤ 2.5 sec
INP ≤ 200 ms
CLS ≤ 0.1

Those are Google's current recommended thresholds.

For your Next.js site I'd pay particular attention to:

image sizes, unnecessary JavaScript, third-party affiliate scripts, analytics scripts, font loading, client components and ad slots.

Your monetisation can easily damage performance if advertising and tracking aren't engineered carefully.

17. My priority roadmap for TradingGuide

If I were planning the next 12 months of development, I'd prioritize the work in roughly this order:

P0 — Foundation: Search Console, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, metadata, schema, Core Web Vitals, indexing audit and analytics.
P0 — Trust: About, methodology, authors, editorial policy, affiliate disclosure, corrections, sources, verification dates and appropriate financial disclosures.
P1 — Data: build a reliable structured database covering roughly 15–30 important Australian platforms and maintain source/verification history.
P1 — Money pages: create excellent provider pages and carefully researched comparisons based on the structured data.
P1 — Topic clusters: build substantial Share Trading, Brokers, ETFs, Forex, Crypto and Education hubs rather than publishing disconnected posts.
P1 — Tools: launch a broker comparison engine, brokerage calculator, FX-fee calculator and broker matcher.
P2 — Content: publish fewer but significantly better Australian-focused guides, continuously updating the ones that begin receiving impressions.
P2 — Authority: publish proprietary Australian trading research and use it for digital PR, citations and natural backlinks.
P3 — Scale: only after the templates, data quality, editorial process and internal-linking system work well should you scale toward hundreds of pages.
The key concern

I would not define success as:

"Publish 1,000 articles → Google page 1."

I'd define it as:

Build the most useful, transparent and data-rich Australian trading-platform comparison resource we realistically can.

Then SEO becomes a consequence of the product rather than the entire product.

Google's own guidance is remarkably close to this philosophy: create content people find genuinely helpful and satisfying rather than creating pages for every possible search variation.

And because your business model ultimately depends on SEO → comparison → affiliate conversion, this approach also makes the business more defensible: the structured provider database powers provider pages, comparison pages, calculators, articles and affiliate journeys simultaneously.
