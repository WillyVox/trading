# 32. Sponsored/promoted distinction needs to stay explicit

Canstar labels promoted placements clearly and explains that ad position doesn't determine its Star Ratings.

Finder also labels promoted products while explaining that methodology is independent.

As your affiliate business develops, make sure we distinguish:

Editorial ranking

from:

Sponsored placement

and:

Affiliate relationship

Sponsored

cards above organic comparison results, label them visibly.
