31. Comparison page methodology should sit near the actual table

I particularly like Finder/Canstar's pattern of explaining:

How we compare

right alongside the comparison experience.

Don't force users to hunt through the footer.

On:

/compare/crypto-exchanges

you could have:

How this comparison works

We compare:
• fees
• AUD deposits
• supported assets
• regulatory information
• platform capabilities

Data sources:
• provider documentation
• fee schedules
• AUSTRAC
• ASIC

Last data review:
8 September 2026

How we make money:
Some providers may pay us a referral commission.
Commercial relationships do not change factual comparison data.

Then link to the full methodology.

This is excellent for both trust and UX.
