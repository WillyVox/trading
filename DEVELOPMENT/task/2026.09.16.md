# 1. Executive Summary

After reviewing the latest source and the current direction of Trading Guide, my main conclusion is:

> **The technical foundation is becoming strong, but the public product positioning and information architecture have not yet caught up with the business vision.**

The application already contains several valuable foundations:

- Next.js architecture
- Prisma/PostgreSQL data model
- Article CMS
- Provider data
- Source/provenance tracking
- Verification status
- Provider fees/features/regulations
- Crypto assets
- Affiliate tracking
- `/go/[partner]`
- Dynamic metadata
- Canonical URLs
- Open Graph metadata
- JSON-LD
- Sitemap generation
- Robots configuration
- Methodology
- Editorial policy
- Affiliate disclosure
- Centralised navigation
- Guide/news infrastructure

These are worth preserving.

The problem is that the public site still communicates something much closer to:

> **A small Australian crypto-exchange research website**

while the intended business is becoming:

> **A beginner-focused Australian education, research and comparison platform for share-trading platforms and cryptocurrency exchanges.**

The next major phase therefore should **not** be another ground-up technical rewrite.

It should be:

> **Trading Guide Information Architecture + Trading Platform Foundation + SEO Migration**

Your current vision is roughly:

> Build an educational resource and useful guide for cryptocurrency and share-trading platforms, specifically tailored for beginners.
