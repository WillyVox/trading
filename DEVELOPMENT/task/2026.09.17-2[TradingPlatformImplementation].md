# 5. Phase 2 — structured pricing

Once the basic offering comparison works, add:

```text
OfferingPricingPlan
OfferingFee
OfferingFeeTier
OfferingFeeCondition
```

and:

```text
FeeCalculationBasis
FeeTierMetric
FeeConditionType
ConditionOperator
```

This is necessary because real Australian brokerage isn't simply:

```ts
brokerageFee: 5;
```

For example, CommSec's current CDIA/Margin Loan settlement schedule is $5 up to $1,000, $10 through $3,000, $19.95 through $10,000, $29.95 through $25,000 and 0.12% above $25,000; settling to another nominated bank account has a different schedule. ([CommSec][3])

Therefore:

```text
OfferingFee
Australian brokerage
        │
        ├── Condition
        │   settlement = CDIA
        │
        └── Tiers
            ├── ≤ $1,000       $5
            ├── ≤ $3,000       $10
            ├── ≤ $10,000      $19.95
            ├── ≤ $25,000      $29.95
            └── > $25,000      0.12%
```

That's why this modelling work matters.

# 32. Main concerns before coding

There are five I want us to actively design around.

**Fee comparability is the biggest.** `$0 brokerage` does not mean zero total cost. FX, spreads, settlement conditions, market-specific charges and eligibility matter. Our UI should never flatten these into misleading headline numbers.
