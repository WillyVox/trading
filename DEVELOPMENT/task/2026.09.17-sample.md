The brokerage figures above need context rather than simply displaying “CMC = $0”. CMC's $0 Australian brokerage is specifically the first buy under $1,000 per security per day; otherwise its Standard pricing is $11 or 0.10%, whichever is greater. Its international pricing currently advertises $0 brokerage for the US, UK, Canada and Japan, with a 0.60% FX spread.

CommSec's Australian online brokerage when settling through a CDIA or CommSec Margin Loan currently runs from $5 through tiered amounts to 0.12% above $25,000. Its standard International Shares account lists US brokerage of US$5 or 0.12%, whichever is greater, and a 0.55% FX conversion fee.

For custody, both can provide CHESS-sponsored Australian holdings. CommSec's international holdings are different: CommSec says the assets are held through a nominee/custody arrangement, with Interactive Brokers LLC appointed as international custodian, while the customer retains beneficial ownership.
