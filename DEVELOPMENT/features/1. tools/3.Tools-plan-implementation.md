Compliance boundary

The design in your requirement is directionally appropriate.

ASIC says factual descriptions of product features or terms can be provided without necessarily giving financial product advice, but presentation that conveys a recommendation can become advice. ASIC also specifically flags affiliate links and the overall circumstances of the communication.

Therefore the calculator should say:

For this hypothetical scenario:

Published brokerage
A$10

not:

CommSec is better for you.

And certainly not:

🏆 Winner: Stake

The calculator should never use:

age
salary
financial goals
risk tolerance
portfolio

to select a provider.

Your requirements explicitly prohibit a ranking engine and personalised “best for you” outcomes.

Current ASIC advertising guidance also focuses on avoiding false or misleading representations by financial-product promoters and publishers.

That reinforces why the proposed source/assumption/exclusion design matters.
