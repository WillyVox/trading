# Legal & commercial launch worksheet

This worksheet separates facts the code can establish from decisions/review that require the business owner or professional adviser.

## Privacy inputs to confirm

- Legal operator name/ABN/address and privacy contact.
- Whether the operator is an APP entity or an exemption applies.
- Actual production hosting, database, email and other processors.
- Countries in which personal information is likely to be processed/stored, where applicable.
- Actual cookie/storage/analytics behaviour in production.
- Retention/deletion and complaint-handling process.

## Terms inputs to confirm

- Financial-information/advice boundary wording.
- Liability/consumer-law wording.
- NSW governing-law wording.
- Third-party/provider and affiliate-link terms.

## Commercial state

Before launch, confirm whether any provider relationship is ACTIVE/APPROVED and compensated. The public Affiliate Disclosure and footer must describe the real state on launch day. Commercial status must never affect factual search ranking or alphabetical comparison ordering.

## Attestation

`LEGAL_CONTENT_APPROVED=true` is an operator attestation after final Terms/Privacy wording and deployment-specific privacy details have actually been approved. It is not a code-generated claim and must not be defaulted to true.
