# How to Trade Crypto

Crypto trading means buying and selling digital assets like Bitcoin and Ethereum on an exchange,
with the goal of profiting from price changes or acquiring assets to hold long-term.

## Choosing an exchange

Look at supported assets, fee structure, and whether the exchange supports your local currency.

## Order types

- **Market orders** execute immediately at the current price.
- **Limit orders** only execute at your specified price or better.

## Key risks

Crypto markets are volatile and largely unregulated compared to traditional finance. Never invest
more than you can afford to lose, and always use strong account security (two-factor
authentication, unique passwords).
