<p>CoinSpot, Swyftx and Independent Reserve are three of the more commonly used exchanges among Australian beginners, but they're built around genuinely different trade-offs. Here's how they actually compare, without picking an overall "winner."</p>
<h2>Fees</h2>
<p>CoinSpot separates its products clearly: 0.1% on its order-book "Markets" product, versus 1% plus spread on its Instant Buy/Sell/Swap screen, plus a 0.1% OTC desk rate for trades of $20,000 AUD or more. Swyftx instead uses one tiered trading fee that starts at 0.6% and can fall to 0.1% as your rolling 30-day volume increases — reaching that lowest tier requires substantial volume (Swyftx's own materials put the bottom tier at $6 million AUD of rolling volume), with a separate spread applied on top. Independent Reserve publishes a maker/taker fee structure, though we're treating its specific published percentages as indicative rather than confirmed, since our own research found them only via third-party review sites rather than Independent Reserve's own fee page directly — check its current schedule before relying on an exact figure.</p>
<h2>Coin selection</h2>
<p>Swyftx advertises the widest range among the three, at more than 410 crypto assets on its own facts page. CoinSpot also offers a broad range. Independent Reserve runs a narrower, more blue-chip-focused selection — useful if you specifically want a simpler, curated list rather than hundreds of options.</p>
<h2>Interface style</h2>
<p>CoinSpot and Swyftx both lead with an AUD-first, beginner-oriented experience, with instant-buy screens front and centre. Independent Reserve leans more toward an order-book-first interface, which can suit someone already comfortable with limit orders but asks more of a first-time user.</p>
<h2>AUD funding</h2>
<p>All three support AUD deposits, though the specific fee-free methods differ — check each provider's current fee page for exactly which deposit method is free versus which carries a charge, since these details can change.</p>

<h2>Who each tends to suit</h2>
<p>CoinSpot suits someone who wants a simple instant-buy experience with the option to move to a cheaper order-book trade later. Swyftx suits someone who wants the widest coin selection with a similarly simple interface. Independent Reserve suits someone already comfortable with order-book trading who prefers a narrower, more curated asset list. None of these is objectively "best" — it depends on what you actually want from the account.</p>
